DASMixer User Guide

DASMixer is a cross-platform desktop application for integrating and comparing peptide identification data from mass spectrometry experiments. It merges de novo sequencing results with database search identifications, performs complete comparative proteomics workflows, and produces publication-ready interactive and static reports.

Developed at the Laboratory of Structural Proteomics, IBMC, Moscow.
